Librarian's Workbench

Find the class Generate the call number Make book cards ready to print

Your productivity tools in one place. Each one keeps its work while you switch, and each can pass its results to the next step.

1

Class Finder

Search or browse the Dewey and Library of Congress outlines to find the right class number.

For example641.5 Cooking
  • Search DDC or LCC by word or number
  • Its LCC/DDC choice sets the Call Number Generator too
  • Browse the full outline tree
2

Call Number Generator

Add the author and title, and get the full call number with its Cutter mark. Print spine labels in batches.

For exampleCIR 823.7 Au933p 1813
  • Cutter-Sanborn and LC Cutter tables
  • Import from Excel or CSV
3

Book Card Maker

Design your book cards once, then fill a whole sheet of them from your list of books.

Card fieldsCall No. · Author · Title · Acc. #
  • Card design and A4 page layout
  • Print-ready sheets

Passing work along

1 → 2In Class Finder, open a class and press Use in Call Number Generator. It starts a new item with that class, ready for the author and title.
2 → 3In Call Number Generator, press Send to Book Card Maker. It sends all items, or only the ticked ones, one card per copy, with the call number, author and title.
