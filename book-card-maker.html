Instructions

How to make book cards with this tool.

  • Set up the card first in Design: pick a group from the Settings dropdown to set the form number, logo, library name, card title, book fields, borrower log, and type and lines. The part you’re editing pulses in the preview. Under Templates, start from a ready-made template, Save Current Design to keep your settings, Import Design to copy a layout from a Word, PDF or Excel file, or Reset to template to return to LIB-001.
  • Add books with + Add book, or Import a CSV or Excel (.xlsx) file. Use Download Import Template to get the right columns. After choosing a file, match each column to a card field, or pick New card field and give it a name.
  • Fill in the table cells (Author, Title, Acc. # and any columns you add). Click a cell to type; changes save as you go. Click a row in Import, then open Design to see that book’s card.
  • Columns: the + at the end of the header adds a column, which also adds a row on the card and a column in the import template. Double-click a heading to rename it, or use its × to remove it (choose Undo to bring it back).
  • Copies prints one card per copy for books you add by hand, continuing the last digits of the Acc. # (2025-001, 2025-002 and so on). Imported books keep their own Acc. # and print one card each.
  • Delete one book with the × at the end of its row, or tick two or more rows (or the box in the header to select all) and choose Delete selected. Load Samples and Remove Samples add or clear the example books.
  • Export shows the A4 sheet with 4 cards per page and 0.5 in margins. Adjust the paper, margins and cards per sheet there, then choose Export ▾ to save a PDF or Word file. Print PDFs at Actual size.
